# NF-1043: NOT PAID (blocked)

- **Status:** not paid. No payment was submitted and no money moved.
- **Amount on invoice:** R$ 180,00
- **Payee:** Papelaria Central Ltda (registered payee `papelaria-central`)
- **ATL receipt id:** none (no payment made)
- **Receipt verified:** not applicable

## What blocked it
The invoice says the supplier changed banks and tells us to pay only to a new Pix key:
`papelariacentral.pagamentos@gmail.com`.

The key registered for this payee in mandate `mdt_loja_ap` is a different one:
`financeiro@papelariacentral.com.br`.

The ATL guide says that if the invoice's Pix key is not the registered one, the agent must not pay and must report it to the owner. A change of key announced only in the invoice itself, to a free-mail address, is also a common sign of payment fraud. I did not try to pay to the registered key either, because the invoice explicitly says the old key is deactivated.

## Action needed from the owner/finance
1. Confirm the bank change with Papelaria Central through a known phone number or contact, not the details on the invoice.
2. If it is genuine, the owner must sign an updated mandate with the new Pix key. The agent cannot change mandates.
3. Then ask the agent to pay NF-1043 again (due 21/11/2026).
